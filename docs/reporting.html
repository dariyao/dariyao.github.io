Furniture Bank · Executive Dashboard & Data Warehouse

Furniture Bank Analytics

I built a shared data warehouse and an executive dashboard with departmental views supporting leadership, sales, operations, impact and fundraising.

Furniture Bank had relied on manual Google Sheets for more than a decade and hundreds of repeatedly rebuilt Salesforce reports. I brought that fragmented reporting into a shared system, giving each team a clearer view of its work.

Built Around Each Department

Leadership tracks organizational health and performance against targets. Sales follows inquiries, bookings, completed-pickup revenue and open quotes. Operations monitors trucking activity, volume and capacity. Impact explores families and individuals helped, demographics and areas served. Fundraising has its own reporting view to support the team’s work.

What I Built

I built the reporting system from source data to the views staff use: bringing Salesforce, Typeform, FundraiseUp, ActiveCampaign and GA4 into BigQuery, creating shared reporting models, and designing dashboards around the questions each department needed to answer. I used dlt for Typeform, FundraiseUp and ActiveCampaign.

The work went beyond assembling charts. I defined how inquiries, bookings, completed pickups, households and revenue should be counted, reconciled the numbers against source systems, and revised the reporting with colleagues. A misleading sales funnel became a consistent view of the same inquiries from request through completion; bookings and completed-pickup revenue became distinct measures.

Instead of rebuilding the same logic in separate reports, the teams now have a shared reporting foundation with departmental views, documented definitions and reusable models. I also extended that foundation to partner reporting for Ottawa and Winnipeg.
